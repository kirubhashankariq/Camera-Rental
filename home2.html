Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need insurance to rent?

Yes, all rentals require a valid Certificate of Insurance (COI) or a credit card deposit equivalent to the replacement value of the gear.

Do you deliver to sets?

We offer direct-to-set delivery within a 50-mile radius of our hub locations. Delivery fees are calculated at checkout based on distance.

What happens if gear is damaged?

If equipment is damaged, please notify us immediately. Your insurance will be responsible for repair or replacement costs. All our gear is rigorously tested before and after every rental.
