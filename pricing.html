Insurance Options

Protecting high-end gear is essential. We offer two straightforward ways to cover your rentals.

Provide Your Own COI

If your production company carries short-term or annual production insurance, you can upload a Certificate of Insurance listing OmniLens as the Loss Payee.

  • - Must cover full replacement value.
  • - Requires 'Unlocked/Unattended Auto' coverage.

Credit Card Deposit

For smaller setups or independent creators without formal insurance, we can place a temporary hold on a major credit card for the replacement value.

  • - Hold is placed 24h before pickup.
  • - Released immediately upon safe return.

How Deposits Work

1. Authorization

A pre-authorization hold is placed on your card 24 hours before your scheduled pickup.

2. The Amount

The hold amount equals the retail replacement value of the specific items you are renting.

3. Inspection

Upon return, our technicians carefully inspect the equipment for any new damages or missing parts.

4. Release

Once cleared, the hold is released immediately. It may take 3-5 days for your bank to reflect this.

Billing FAQ

Do you offer student discounts?

Yes! We offer a 15% discount for active film students. Please create an account using your .edu email address and upload your valid student ID in your dashboard.

When am I charged for the rental?

The actual rental fee is charged at the time of booking to reserve the gear. The separate security deposit hold is authorized 24 hours prior to pickup.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations made 48 hours before pickup receive a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours are subject to a 50% cancellation fee.

Need a Custom Quote?

Shooting a feature over several months? Need a massive multi-cam setup? We provide highly competitive custom quotes for large productions.
