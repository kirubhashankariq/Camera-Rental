How It Works

Getting industry-leading equipment shouldn't be complicated. We've streamlined the entire rental process from booking to wrap.

1

Explore our live catalog of pristine cinema cameras, lenses, and lighting. Add items to your cart, select your shooting dates using our interactive calendar, and upload your COI or select a card hold.

Choose to pick up your gear from our local hubs or have it delivered directly to your set. Every item is packed in custom hard-cases. Scan the QR code on the case to view the prep checklist and verify the contents.
